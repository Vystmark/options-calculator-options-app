Prices: midpoint of bid and ask when a live chain is loaded, otherwise Black-Scholes-Merton estimates. Scenario curves reprice every leg with its own implied volatility and time left. Assumes European-style exercise (no early assignment), a constant dividend yield, and no commissions or margin interest. Chance of profit uses a lognormal distribution at the at-the-money implied volatility. For education and planning, not investment advice.
